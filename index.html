About Your Guide

A practice rooted
in compassion

I've spent over a decade exploring the healing arts — from mindfulness-based stress reduction to somatic bodywork, breathwork, and energy healing. Green Leaf Healing grew from my own journey back to wholeness.

I believe that stillness is not something we find outside ourselves — it's something we remember. My role is simply to help you remember.

Each session, recording, and resource I create is made with the sincere intention of helping you feel a little more at home in yourself.
